projects

Urban Inequality in Vadodara

Thesis project, April 2026

  • Built a 0–100 Urban Opportunity Index for each ward, combining access to healthcare, schools, roads and buses with flood exposure. Travel times came from OpenStreetMap road networks and Vadodara Smart City bus data.
  • Measured how unevenly opportunity is spread using Moran’s I, LISA cluster maps, Gini and Lorenz curves and a Kolm–Pollak equality-adjusted score, then added Bayesian spatial models (BYM2/ICAR).
  • Cleaned and merged all inputs into reproducible CSV and GeoPackage files, and published choropleth maps with an interactive dashboard.
